# Release notes, build 8 (Android): draft for her OK

Since the first internal build (versionCode 1, 2026-10-28). Build 8 ships as versionCode 8 (the PM's decision, as iOS's
build numbers; set in this commit), then +1 per upload. Nothing is uploaded without her yes.

## Play "What's new" (under 500 characters)

Your drafts now save as you go, and you can add, edit or delete notes any time.
Upload a PDF, Word or text file and it's read on your phone, scans included.
Add a recovery email, link a second phone, or see and sign out your phones.
Submitting is safer: a retry never charges twice.
Accepting a delivery asks first.
Larger text, screen-reader labels and bigger buttons throughout.
The app opens straight to your drafts, even on a slow connection.

## For testers (fuller list)

- **Describing a project:** drafts save while you type, speak or upload. A draft without a brief takes more notes ("+"),
  and notes can be edited or deleted. Drafts are named from the first note. Swipe or long-press a draft to delete it.
- **Documents:** PDF, Word, RTF, text and Markdown are read on the phone. The file itself stays on your phone. Scanned PDFs are read
  page by page with progress, and "Stop reading" stops them. A file still downloading from the cloud shows "Getting
  <name>" with Stop.
- **The brief:** an AI job keeps going if you close the app, and its result is picked up when you come back.
- **Your account:**
  - Recovery email, link codes for a second phone, a list of the phones with access, and "Sign out this phone".
  - The recovery key stays out of screenshots and Recents, and is copied for a minute only.
- **Money:** a submit whose answer was lost is retried exactly as sent and never charged twice. A purchase that the
  store confirmed but that isn't booked yet says so, and syncs the next time you open Credits.
- **After delivery:**
  - Accept asks first.
  - A revision request isn't lost if sending fails.
  - The transfer line names the GitHub account the code went to.
  - A failed transfer points to support.
- **Everywhere:** larger text sizes, TalkBack labels, 48dp tap targets, clearer error lines, a faster start that opens
  from your phone's copy, and many fixes from the QA suite and two independent reviews.
